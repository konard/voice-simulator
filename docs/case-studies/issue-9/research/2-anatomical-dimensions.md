**Main gaps (not found):** vallecula dimensions; total external uvula length; absolute tongue length, width and height; hyoid coordinates; a female epilarynx value; and child values for the piriform fossa, incisors and soft-tissue profile.

**Caveats:**

- For adults, Vorperian cohort IV (15–19 y) is a proxy; true adults may run slightly larger.
- Perry's adult velum and palate values pool both sexes.
- Kollara's child "SD" may be standard error.
- Bonneau's lip data pool sexes and come from French speakers.
