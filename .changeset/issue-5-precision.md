Increase the precision of the simulation (issue 5). The 3D FDTD tiers render the same waveform about 1.5 to 1.7 times faster with a WebAssembly kernel, and the app notices within the first 40-300 ms of an utterance when a tier cannot keep up, restarting it on a cheaper tier before any gap is audible. The head gains teeth, a tongue and a hard and soft palate, both in the 3D view (with levels of detail) and in the acoustics (incisors and piriform fossae). The microphone can be placed anywhere around the head, with an optional room reverb or a simulated box of air. Male, female and child presets, 32 head parameters covering everything that changes the sound, and voice matching from a recording (`matchVoice`, `voice-simulator match`) are added. Audio can be written and read as WAV (8-32-bit integer, 32/64-bit float), FLAC, AIFF, AU and raw PCM. The previous version of the web app stays online at /v1/.
