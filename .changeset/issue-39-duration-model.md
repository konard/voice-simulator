Time every sound with the segment duration rules of Klatt (1979) and a table per language (issue 39). `src/articulation/durations.js` exports `segmentDuration()`, `durationFeatures()`, `minimumDuration()`, `durationFormula()`, `scalePause()`, `durationTable()`, `DURATION_RULES` (end of a clause or phrase, position in the word, number of syllables, stress, emphasis, the next consonant, clusters, vowel sequences, Russian vowel reduction in two degrees, Hindi vowel length, the voice onset after aspirated plosives, Mandarin tones with a short neutral tone) and `DURATION_TABLES` (`en`, `ru`, `hi`, `zh`, `default`) with inherent and minimum durations per sound class and symbol. `speechRate` shortens the minimum with the exponent `RATE_MINIMUM = 0.5`, the rest with `RATE_STRETCH = 1.2` and the pauses at phrase boundaries with `RATE_PAUSE = 1.5`. The fixed 0.105 s base duration and the old lengthening code are gone; `planUtterance()` takes a `durationTable` option, and `dtw()` takes `diagonalWeight`. `experiments/issue-11-fit-durations.mjs` fits the inherent durations to the golden set: the error per sound falls by 39 % for Hindi but by only 0.9 % for English and 8.8 % for Russian. The audit sentence is 3.723 s long (3.980 s before), and voice matching uses the speech-rate slopes 0.28 (syllable rate) and 0.75 (speaking time) measured under the new rules.
