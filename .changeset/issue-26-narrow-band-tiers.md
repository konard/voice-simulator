Add three audio tiers below `minimal` (issue 26): `telephone` (waveguide, 12 sections, 8000 Hz), `narrowband` (16 sections, 11025 Hz) and `wideband` (22 sections, 16000 Hz). Each has a `blockSize` near 1.5 ms and a `bandLimit` (3400, 4800, 7000 Hz): the output stage then filters with an eighth-order Butterworth low-pass at the solver rate and once more at the output rate, so the output keeps F1 to F3 and has no aliasing. Tiers without a `bandLimit` give bit-identical samples. New exports: `audioTierIndex(name)` and `BASE_AUDIO_LEVEL` (the index of `minimal`, where automatic quality starts in the example app). `synthesize` and `prepareUtterance` accept `blockSize` and `bandLimit`. `analyzeVoice` takes `formantCount`, which defaults to 3 at 8 kHz and to 4 from 11025 Hz; formants it does not measure are 0. The server accepts the sample rates 8000 and 11025, and renders a narrow-band tier at its own rate when the request gives no `sampleRate`. `measureAudioTier` and `benchmarkAudioTiers` take `repeats` (median of that many measurements) and `benchmarkAudioTiers` takes `warmUp` (seconds of untimed rendering first); `voice-simulator benchmark` uses 5 repeats and 0.5 s of warm-up, so the tier measured first no longer pays for the just-in-time compilation.
