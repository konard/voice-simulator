Make live recognition read each phrase with the word decoder, like `recognize()`, instead of spelling the phone decoder's output, so the stream commits the same words as a batch read. Keep noise from producing text: a phrase now needs `minVoiced` seconds of pitch-voiced speech (0.1 s in a stream by default, opt-in for `recognize()`). Add `createNoise` and `addNoise` with ten noise types (white, pink, brown, blue, violet, speech-shaped, babble, hum, crackle and drifting), and `scripts/noise-baseline.mjs`, which measures recognition of synthesized speech in each noise at several SNRs (`docs/reports/noise-baseline.md`). Pass breaths, the voice source, source-tract interaction, subglottal coupling and headroom through the synthesis worker. The example app shows speech settings (accent level, source, headroom, weak forms, breaths, interaction, subglottal coupling) and recognition settings (level, decoder, unknown words, whisper, modes, speech gate, microphone processing, and noise over a file at a chosen SNR), an input level meter, recognition of the last spoken audio, and a download of the recognized input.
