Add the streaming analysis front end of reverse simulation (issue 58). `createFrameAnalyzer(sampleRate, options)` resamples the input to a working rate of 16000, 11025 or 8000 Hz (`workingRate`) chunk by chunk with the new `createResampler`, which matches `resample()` within 1e-6, and fills one frame set of flat `Float32Array`s every 5 or 10 ms with energy, zero crossings, centre of gravity, spectral tilt, six Liu (1996) bands, periodicity (Boersma 1993), 24 log mel energies, 13 cepstra, LPC coefficients, reflection coefficients and gain, a minimum-statistics noise floor (Martin 2001) and a speech flag with hangover; `push`, `flush`, `trim` and `reset` allocate nothing per frame, and `analyzeFrames` analyses a whole recording in one call. The defaults are `FRONTEND_DEFAULTS`. `createFft(size)` is the one transform of the package (`fft()` of the comparison metrics uses it), and `lpcBurg`, `lpcAutocorrelation`, `lpcCovariance`, `predictorFromReflection` and `lpcLevel` are exported with types. The noise floor is capped at the loudest speech frame minus 25 dB, so noise at the start of a recording is not called speech (`node experiments/issue-58-noise-cap.mjs`). Measured with `node experiments/issue-58-rtf.mjs`: 0.0138 of real time at 16000 Hz and 0.0059 at 8000 Hz; `node experiments/issue-58-delay.mjs` gives at most 13.5 ms of added delay.
