Add the formant tracker of reverse simulation (issue 60). `trackFormants(frames, options)` and the stage `createFormantTracker(frames, options)` read the frame set of the analysis front end and give F1 to F4 (F1 to F3 at 8000 Hz) per frame: the roots of the LPC models of orders p, p - 2 and p - 4 are the candidates, a dynamic programming search numbers them against the resonances of a uniform tube of the tract length (Talkin 1987), and a Kalman filter with a Rauch-Tung-Striebel smoother gives continuous tracks with a variance. In a stream `advance()` fixes frame t when frame t + `lag` has been scored (10 frames, 50 ms). The track holds frequency, bandwidth, variance, level and validity per formant, a nasal flag per frame and the tract length, given as `vocalTractLength` or estimated from vowel-like frames (`tractLengthFromFormants`). The defaults are `FORMANT_DEFAULTS`; everything is typed. The tests estimate 13, 16.5 and 19 cm tracts within 8 % and the tracker costs about 0.03 of real time at 16000 Hz; on the golden set 99.6 % of the voiced frames have F1 to F3 and 0.4 % of the F2 steps jump by more than 300 Hz (docs/reports/recognition-formants.md).
