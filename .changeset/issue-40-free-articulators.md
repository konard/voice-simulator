Move the jaw, the lips, the larynx and the tongue root on their own (issue 40). `planUtterance()` has four more channels in `ARTICULATORS`: `jaw` (gap between the incisors, cm, 0.1 to 1.8, time constant 0.02 s; the planner writes `teethGap()` of each sound), `lipProtrusion` (cm, -0.3 to 1.2, 0.02 s; `LIP_PROTRUSION` = 0.6 times the rounding, exported from `tract-shape.js`), `larynxHeight` (cm, -1.5 to 1.5, 0.04 s) and `tongueRoot` (-1 to 1, 0.03 s). `tractShape()` uses each channel's difference from the planner's value, so the default channels give the same shape: a wider jaw scales the mouth by `1 + JAW_AREA * (jaw - expected)` (`JAW_AREA` = 0.8 per cm, F1 of `[ɛ]` +9 % for 0.5 cm; the tongue and the lips make up for the jaw while they form a consonant), protrusion lengthens the last `LIP_REGION` = 1.5 cm and a lower larynx the first `LARYNX_REGION` = 3 cm (`stretchSections()` keeps the volume; the velum, the teeth and the noise source keep their distance from the lips), and the tongue root scales the pharynx (`TONGUE_ROOT_AREA` = 0.4). New head parameters `jawPosture`, `lipPosture`, `larynxPosture`, `tongueRootPosture` (mouth) and `intrinsicPitch`, `larynxPitch` (prosody, off by default). `faceRig()` reads `lipProtrusion` and returns `larynxHeight`. The 3D voxel tract goes on below the glottis by `LARYNX_TRAVEL` = 0.25 of its length and the source plane moves along it in whole cells with the tract length (`stepDuration()` of `[u]` and `[æ]` differ by 3.6 % at `dx` 1, against 10.8 %); the piriform fossae and the vallecula move with the larynx, and the kernel skips the closed links below the glottis. The 3D vowel table is refitted (`VOWEL_LENGTH_SCALE_3D` = 0.8369). `matchVoice` reads the tract length from the spacing of F1 to F3 (`lowerFormantLength()`), which follows the length where the reading with F4 reverses.
