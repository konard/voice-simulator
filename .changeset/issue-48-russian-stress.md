Stress Russian words outside the small dictionary and choose the stress of homographs by context (issue 48). `data/lexicon/ru-stress.txt` now holds 6 639 stems with their stress (`раб+от|а`, `стол|+` for stress on the ending) and 3 691 whole forms, written by hand for the most frequent words of Common Voice Russian text (CC0-1.0); inflected forms are found by stem and ending (`телефонами`, source `morphology`). 67 homograph spellings take the reading their neighbours point to (`ключ от замка` zɐmˈka, `на горе` ɡɐˈrʲe, `два часа` t͡ɕɪˈsa, `всё равно`), `е` is read as `ё` when the lexicon knows only that word (`еще` jɪˈɕːo), a typed `+` before a vowel or U+0301 after it always wins (`з+амок`), and unknown words are guessed from their ending by a table counted on the data (74.0 % right on the held-out fifth). On the 222 hand-marked sentences of `tests/fixtures/russian-stress.txt`, 97.9 % of words and 96.0 % of homograph words get the right stress (52.6 % and 36.0 % before; 87.6 % without the 108 lexicon items and 3 homographs added after measuring the fixture). The `ru.js` lexicon grows from 3 541 to 58 979 bytes; the `vslex` container can store the letters of its words in a new `letters` field, so that Cyrillic words are coded as compactly as ASCII ones. New exports `findRussianStress()`, `guessRussianStress()`, `RUSSIAN_ENDINGS`, `RUSSIAN_HOMOGRAPHS`, `russianHomograph()`, `isRussianHomograph()`, `GENITIVE_BEFORE` and `PLURAL_BEFORE`; `lexiconFor('ru', 'forms' | 'stems')`.
