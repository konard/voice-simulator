Speech markup (issue 50). Text that starts with `<speak` is read as an SSML subset (`speak`, `p`, `s`, `break`, `prosody` with rate, pitch, range, volume and `vs:gain`, `emphasis`, `say-as`, `phoneme`, `lang`, `sub`, and `vs:effort`, `vs:phonation`, `vs:emotion`, `vs:sound`); with `markup: true` (CLI `--markup`, server `markup=true`) inline tags such as `[whisper]…[/whisper]`, `[fast]`, `[pitch +2]`, `[pause 0.5]`, `[laugh]` and `*word*` are read; `markup: false` (CLI `--plain`) reads everything as plain text. IPA strings take ExtIPA/VoQS marks: `{W … W}`, `{f … f}`, `{allegro … allegro}`, `{V̤ … V̤}`, `(.)`, `(1.2)`, `⸨laugh⸩` and `ˈˈ`. All three become mode spans; `textToIpa` returns `modes` and `warnings` and writes the marks into `ipa`, and the plan gets `plan.modes` (spans in seconds, `modeAt(plan, time)`) and `plan.sounds`. Rate, pitch, range, gain and effort change the sound, timed pauses last exactly their length and a sound is silence for now. The SSML reader fetches nothing and expands no entities; DOCTYPE, CDATA, processing instructions and inputs over `MARKUP_LIMITS` throw `MarkupError` (400 in the server). New exports: `parseMarkup`, `isSsml`, `createMode`, `composeMode`, `modeAt`, `MarkupError`, `MARKUP_LIMITS`, `MARKUP_NAMESPACE`, `MODE_DEFAULTS`, `SOUND_NAMES`, `SOUND_DURATION`, `EMOTIONS`. Text without markup is unchanged. See docs/markup.md.
