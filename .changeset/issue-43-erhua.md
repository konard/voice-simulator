Speak Mandarin erhua as one syllable (issue 43): 儿 after a syllable joins it with an r-coloured rime (花儿 `xwaɻ˥`, 一点儿 `i˥˩ tjaɻ˨˩˦`, 事儿 `ʂəɻ˥˩`), while 儿 stays a syllable of its own at the start of a word and in words such as 儿子 and 女儿. Pinyin with erhua (`huār`, `hua1r`, `huar1`, `yìdiǎnr`) is read as Chinese instead of English, and the timeline highlights 花儿 as one syllable.
