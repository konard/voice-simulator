Move the articulators like damped masses that approach targets (issue 38). Every articulation channel of `planUtterance()` is now a list of targets `{value, start, end, strength}` (`channelTargets(segments, name)`) that `approachTargets()` of `src/articulation/dynamics.js` follows with `DYNAMICS_ORDER = 2` one-pole filters forwards in time, a critically damped system without overshoot, so short sounds undershoot their targets. `ARTICULATORS` replaces `CHANNELS` (kept as a deprecated export) with `{timeConstant, lead, min, max}` per channel: the lead is 1.68 time constants, which keeps the middle of each transition at the boundary, except that the lips round `ROUNDING_LEAD = 0.12` s and the velum opens `VELUM_LEAD = 0.08` s before their sound, never before the end of the previous sound with its own target. During a consonant the vowel channels take `r * own + (1 - r) * line` with the resistance `r` of `COARTICULATION_RESISTANCE` (0 for labials and glottals, 0.3 for alveolars, 0.6 for `[s z]` and velars, 1 for palatals, `[ʃ ʒ]`, trills and dark `[ɫ]`). New `planUtterance()` options `durations` (seconds per token), `articulators` and `resistance`, and a head parameter `coarticulation` (factor on the leads, 0.5 to 2). `experiments/issue-11-fit-dynamics.mjs` fits the dynamics to the golden set through `alignToRecording()` of `experiments/issue-11-align.mjs`; the held-out formant error fell by 3.1 %, less than the 5 % required, so the starting values are kept. The audit sentence changes by at most 0.57 dB at the same duration, and `planUtterance()` takes 5 % longer.
