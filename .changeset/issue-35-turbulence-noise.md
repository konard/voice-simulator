'voice-simulator': minor

Drive frication, aspiration and breath noise by the air flow: the new head parameter `lungPressure` pushes air through the glottis and the narrowest place of the mouth, the mouth pressure follows with the lag of the oral compliance, and the noise at each orifice grows as dp^1.5 A^0.5 (Stevens 1971) above a critical Reynolds number, in a band centred on the Strouhal frequency of the jet. The release of a stop adds a burst transient from the flow derivative, and voiceless obstruents open the glottis. [s] now peaks near 7 kHz, 1.2 kHz above [ʃ], and aspirated stops get a voice onset time of about 60 ms. The air flow and the source strengths are exported (`Aerodynamics`, `sourceStrength`, `steadyFlow`, `strouhalFrequency`, ...) and ported to the Rust core with bit-identical parity tests; the lag of the mouth pressure uses a rational form of 1 - exp(-x) (`lagFactor`) so that Node.js, Deno and Bun render the same samples. With `source: 'folds'` the glottal area of the air flow is the new `VocalFolds.meanArea` (three one-pole stages of 5 ms, `MEAN_AREA_STAGES` and `MEAN_AREA_TIME`), and the breath noise of the folds follows the source strength of that area.

`matchVoice` corrects its breathiness step for the change of the speech rate, since faster speech reads a noisier voice at the same breathiness, so a first render at the wrong rate no longer pushes the breathiness up: breathiness 0.1 is recovered as 0.2 instead of 0.31.
