Vocal effort and loudness dynamics (issue 52). The effort of a mode span (`[soft]`, `[loud]`, `[shout]`, `{p … p}`, `<vs:effort>`, `<prosody volume>`) now follows the rules of `EFFORT_RULES`: at `E = (effort - 0.5) * 40` dB the level changes by `E`, the lung pressure by `2^(E/9)`, the pitch by +5 Hz per dB (-4 … +12 st), F1 by +3.5 Hz per dB, the tilt above 1 kHz by `0.2 E` dB, vowels (not consonants) get 1.1 and 1.3 times longer at +10 and +20 dB and the noise sources change by `0.5 E` dB. The plan gets `effort` and `tension` channels; the fold source takes level and tilt from the lung pressure and keeps the pitch rule (`FOLD_PITCH_RISE`, `foldPitchFactor()`). New inline tags `[crescendo]`, `[diminuendo]` and `[lombard]` (also `<vs:effort level="lombard">`): ramps run linearly in dB from the first to the last vowel and warn above 40 dB/s; Lombard speech lengthens only stressed vowels. The named SSML volumes are now the steps of the dynamics marks (`x-soft` -20, `soft` -10, `loud` +10, `x-loud` +20 dB of effort), so `<prosody volume="soft">` is written `{p … p}`. The output is lowered by the loudest span (`levelOffsetDb` in the result, `headroom` option, CLI `--headroom`) so that `[shout]` does not clip; `loudnessTarget` (CLI `--loudness`) normalises to an ITU-R BS.1770 integrated loudness with a peak of at most 0.98. New exports: `EFFORT_RULES`, `EFFORT_PITCH_LIMITS`, `EFFORT_RAMP_LIMIT`, `FOLD_PITCH_RISE`, `effortChanges`, `effortToDecibels`, `decibelsToEffort`, `effortToSpl`, `splToEffort`, `fastEffortRamps`, `foldPitchFactor`, `maxModeLevel`, `levelDb`, `alphaRatio`, `kWeighting`, `integratedLoudness`, `normalizeLoudness`. The Rust engine realises the same gains (interface version 3: frames carry `effort` and `tension`). Text without markup gives the same samples as before. See docs/markup.md.
